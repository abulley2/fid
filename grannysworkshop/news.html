New Game in Production!

Grandma’s Workshop Announces New Whimsical Mystery Video Game: Who Took Mr. Bubbles?

Grandma’s Workshop Game Studios has officially revealed its newest project in development: Who Took Mr. Bubbles?, a cozy, family‑friendly mystery video game centered around the disappearance of a beloved goldfish.

In this narrative‑driven deduction adventure, players join a quirky cast of characters as they investigate the sudden vanishing of Mr. Bubbles, the household’s treasured goldfish. The studio describes the game as a “digital mystery board game brought to life,” blending exploration, clue‑gathering, and lighthearted interrogation.

Gameplay unfolds across a colorful, hand‑drawn map filled with potential hiding spots, suspicious characters, and humorous red herrings. Players take turns asking questions, uncovering clues, and eliminating possibilities. With each round, the game narrows down the suspects and locations until only one character and one final hiding place remain — revealing who “took” Mr. Bubbles and why.

Developers say the goal is to recreate the feeling of solving a mystery with family on the couch, but with the added charm of animated storytelling, expressive characters, and interactive environments. True to Grandma’s Workshop’s roots, the game emphasizes warmth, humor, and the joy of shared discovery.

While no release date has been announced, early concept art and prototype footage have already sparked excitement among fans of cozy mystery games and narrative puzzlers. If the studio’s past titles are any indication, Who Took Mr. Bubbles? is shaping up to be another heartfelt addition to their story‑inspired lineup.

Mr. Bubbles may be missing — but the anticipation for his digital debut is very much alive.
