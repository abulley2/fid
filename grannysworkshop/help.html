Having issuses with our games?

heres some Common problems

The game won’t launch. What should I do?

First, make sure your graphics drivers are up to date. Then verify your game files through your launcher to repair anything corrupted. If the game still won’t open, check that your antivirus isn’t blocking it and confirm your device meets the minimum system requirements.

I’m having audio problems — no sound or missing effects. How do I fix it?

Double‑check your audio output device and update your sound drivers. Restart the game after plugging in new headphones or speakers. If audio still isn’t working, reset your in‑game audio settings to default.

I can’t connect to online features. What’s wrong?

Restart your router or try a wired connection. Make sure the game servers are online and that your game is fully updated. VPNs and proxies can also cause connection issues, so try disabling them.

I’m stuck or confused about how to progress. Any tips?

Check the in‑game tutorial or “How to Play” section for guidance. Many players find the hint system helpful if you want a nudge without spoilers. You can also review the map or UI markers for clues on where to go next.

I’m confused about part of the story. Is this a bug or intentional?

Many story moments are designed to be mysterious, but if something feels off, you can check our spoiler‑free hint section or story FAQ. We also include clarifications in patch notes when needed.
