For over twenty years, Grandma’s Workshop Game Studios has been dedicated to bringing heartfelt stories to life. Founded by Olivia Hermann, the studio began with a simple dream: to preserve and share the imaginative tales passed down from her grandmother. What started as a personal tribute has grown into a creative home for cozy, narrative‑driven games that celebrate family, storytelling, and the magic of childhood wonder.

Every game we create is inspired by real stories or new adventures imagined in the spirit of Grandma’s original tales. Whether it’s a mystery, a journey, or a whimsical slice‑of‑life adventure, our goal is always the same — to recreate the feeling of sitting beside a grandparent as they tell a captivating story, full of warmth, humor, and heart.

Grandma’s Workshop is proudly based in Michigan, where our small but passionate team works to craft experiences that bring people together. From concept art to final polish, every detail is shaped with care and a deep respect for the storytelling traditions that inspired us.
