Boss Battle

made in 2023
Colored pencils on paper

Boss battle took second place in an art contest back when it was first made. It was also apart of a color theroy study where i wanted a battle between complementary colors. It was made so that the game felt vivd and alive while the computer around it was dull and in muted hues. the piece has since inspired me to make more are pices with the same charater running across new adventures.
